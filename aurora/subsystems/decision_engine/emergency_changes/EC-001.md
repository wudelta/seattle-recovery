# Emergency Change EC-001

## Status

RESOLVED

## Trigger

Planning Step #421 could not complete because the Step had no open repository
baseline. Planning correctly refused to attribute repository changes without
that baseline.

During normal recovery, the worker encountered repeated process-navigation and
invocation gaps that caused the recovery process itself to prevent forward
engineering progress.

## Authorization

Explicit human authorization was granted on 2026-09-07.

Emergency authority applies only to the minimum work necessary to diagnose,
repair, validate, and document the control-plane failure preventing Step #421
from completing correctly.

Creation and maintenance of this Emergency Change record and its durable
Decision Engine directory are explicitly authorized under EC-001 because they
are required to preserve the emergency recovery evidence itself.

## Governing Principle

> When process prevents correct work, the process has become its own defect.

Emergency Change does not suspend Aurora's normal authorities generally.

It permits the minimum necessary exception when those authorities cannot safely
represent or permit the recovery required to restore normal operation.
